06 · FAQ

Reasonable questions. Dry answers.

Is she really an AI?+
Yes. She says so in her first sentence, on every call. There is no trick, which is its own kind of trick.
Can she transfer to a human?+
Whenever a caller asks, or a call needs judgement, she hands off with the full context. She does not pretend to be the human, and she does not hide one from you either.
Do I have to change my number?+
No. Keep your existing number or take a new Twilio number. She answers either. The one thing she will not do is ask your customers to press one for sales.
Does she actually book meetings?+
Into your calendar, with confirmations and follow-ups. The no-show rate is not zero. It is lower than yours.
What happens to my data?+
Calls and transcripts belong to you. They run your agent, not a model you do not control. She forgets nothing and shares nothing.
How fast can she go live?+
Days, not months. The slowest part is usually deciding what she should say. She already knows how to say it.
